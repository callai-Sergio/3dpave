How it works

  • Same shapes, sampling and initial positions as Marshall Simulator for the same seed.
  • The mastic (binder + fines < 2 mm) is a film on the aggregates, with the volume M from the composition. Where films touch there is a cohesive force: the mix forms a mass and falls as a lump, not grain by grain.
  • 1) The aggregates are gathered into a loose mass (AVC ≈ 40%), as in the mixing bowl. 2) The mass drops 30 mm into the mould, with gravity and friction 0.1. 3) The hammer strikes face 1; the mould is turned over (gravity reversed) and face 2 is struck.
  • The hammer is a free plate with the 4.536 kg mass scaled to the specimen mass; it arrives at 0.5 m/s and rests until the material stops. The real energy (3 m/s) destabilises the soft DEM contacts.
  • Without the viscosity of hot binder, the blows do not reach the target AVC. 4) So, at the end, the specimen is pressed to the target-AVC height, as in the final stage of Wan et al. (2024), and the film returns to the app thickness. 5) Mastic, voids and bubbles are painted by the main app code.
